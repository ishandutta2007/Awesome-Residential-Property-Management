# Awesome-Residential-Property-Management

## Top Residential Property Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on Residential Rentals, Tenant Portals, Rent Collection, Maintenance, Leasing & Landlord Accounting*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Residential Property Management**. These systems help landlords and property managers handle single-family homes, multi-family units, tenant screening, leases, rent collection, maintenance work orders, and owner reporting.



**Examples** include Buildium, AppFolio, DoorLoop, TenantCloud, Rentec Direct, Avail, Hemlane, Innago, Propertyware, and TurboTenant (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full-featured residential property management platforms remain almost entirely commercial. Open options exist but are limited in maturity and feature depth compared with leading SaaS products. This section lists the strongest available projects and is realistic about the gap.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Buildium](https://www.buildium.com/)**  

  Comprehensive residential property management platform with strong accounting, leasing, tenant portals, and scalability for growing portfolios.



- **[AppFolio](https://www.appfolio.com/)**  

  Feature-rich, AI-enhanced property management platform popular with professional managers for automation, marketing, and operations.



- **[DoorLoop](https://www.doorloop.com/)**  

  Modern all-in-one residential property management platform known for ease of use, transparent pricing, and solid mid-market capabilities.



- **[TenantCloud](https://www.tenantcloud.com/)**  

  Cloud-based property management platform offering free and paid tiers for listings, screening, accounting, and maintenance.



- **[Rentec Direct](https://www.rentecdirect.com/)**  

  Full-featured residential property management software with robust accounting and tenant management tools.



- **[Avail (by Realtor.com)](https://www.avail.co/)**  

  Landlord-focused platform providing listing, tenant screening, rent collection, and lease tools — popular with smaller portfolios.



- **[Hemlane](https://www.hemlane.com/)**  

  Property management platform that combines software with optional full-service management support for residential landlords.



- **[Innago](https://innago.com/)**  

  Free-to-use residential property management software focused on rent collection, leasing, and tenant communication.



- **[Propertyware](https://www.propertyware.com/)**  

  Property management platform especially strong for single-family and scattered-site residential portfolios.



- **[TurboTenant](https://www.turbotenant.com/)**  

  Landlord platform offering free core tools for listings, applications, screening, and rent collection with paid upgrades.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Evaluating **OpenProperty** or similar self-hosted projects when data ownership and customization are top priorities.

- Using Odoo or general ERP modules as a foundation for more complex multi-entity or accounting needs.

- Combining open lease/tenant trackers with separate payment processors, screening services, and accounting tools for small portfolios.

- Accepting that mature tenant screening integrations, automated rent collection at scale, polished mobile apps, and advanced owner portals still favor commercial platforms.

- Starting with free or low-cost commercial tools (Avail, Innago, TurboTenant, TenantCloud free tiers) before investing heavily in open-source customization.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Self-host an open property manager → track units, tenants, and leases → handle rent and basic maintenance → integrate external payments and screening → produce owner reports via exports or simple dashboards. This approach can work for smaller residential portfolios with technical capacity. Commercial platforms (Buildium, AppFolio, DoorLoop, Propertyware, Rentec Direct, TenantCloud, etc.) remain the practical choice for most professional residential managers who need reliability, compliance features, and support without heavy custom development.
